Lonsdaleite is a hexagonal-diamond lattice, meteorite-only material with the same raw combat, mining and defense stats as Voidshard, but a dramatically tougher crystal structure - its tools and armor carry roughly double Voidshard's durability.

Generation: Overworld only, and only inside a Crashed Meteorite structure. Six separate ore blobs are buried inside the meteorite mass itself, replacing Meteorite Block/Core near the mass's core - you're digging through meteorite rock to reach them, not finding it in normal caves. It cannot be found any other way.

Drops: 1 Raw Lonsdaleite per block, boosted by Fortune, or the ore block itself when mined with Silk Touch.

Tool stats: 6200 durability, 25.0 mining speed, +15.0 attack damage bonus, enchantability 28. It is not correct for Netherite-tier-only blocks, the mod's top tier gate. All Lonsdaleite tools and armor are fully enchantable.

Armor: Helmet 4 defense (638 durability), Chestplate 13 defense (928 durability), Leggings 9 defense (870 durability), Boots 3 defense (754 durability). Enchantability 27, toughness 3.8, knockback resistance 22%. Each worn piece reduces explosion damage taken by 17.5%, stacking up to a 70% reduction with the full four-piece set.

Best used for: players who want Voidshard-tier combat and mining performance with far less frequent repairing, at the cost of hunting down a Crashed Meteorite first.
