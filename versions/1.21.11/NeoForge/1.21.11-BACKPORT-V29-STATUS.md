# Useful Ores 1.21.11 NeoForge V29

Fixes custom colored regular fire rendering on top of Useful Ores blocks/ores.

Root cause: unlike the custom rails/campfires fixed in V28, regular colored fire is a multipart blockstate whose actual leaf models are `*_floor*`, `*_side*`, `*_side_alt*`, and `*_up*`. They previously had no explicit 1.21.11 `render_type`, so the custom fire quads could enter the wrong terrain render path even though the blockstate and textures matched vanilla fire.

V29 adds `"render_type": "minecraft:cutout"` to every regular colored-fire leaf model (all 15 colors, 10 leaf model variants each). Textures and blockstate geometry are unchanged.

No changes to campfires, rails, furnace, or relay logic in this batch.
