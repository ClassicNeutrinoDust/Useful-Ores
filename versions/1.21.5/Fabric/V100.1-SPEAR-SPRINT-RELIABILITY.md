# V100.1 — Spear Sprint Reliability

## Problem
V100 is functionally correct, but ordinary ground sprinting can sit close to the vanilla 4.6 blocks/second kinetic-damage threshold. Small sideways velocity, camera interpolation, or collision deflection can lower the **view-axis projection** just enough to reject an otherwise valid ray contact. Speed I increases the velocity margin and therefore masks the issue.

## Fix
V100.1 separates **hit eligibility** from **damage scaling**:

- The existing server-authoritative spear ray remains the sole target/contact detector.
- Damage eligibility uses the magnitude of attacker-relative motion against the unchanged 4.6 blocks/second threshold.
- Actual kinetic damage still uses the directional projection onto the attacker's view vector, preserving speed/aim scaling.
- No client victim packet is used to trigger damage.
- Creative flight behavior, charge timing, jab animation, and existing spear movement remain unchanged.

This is intentionally a reliability-oriented backport adjustment rather than a change to the damage multiplier or sprint speed.
