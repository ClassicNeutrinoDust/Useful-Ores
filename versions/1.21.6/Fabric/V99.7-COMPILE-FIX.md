V99.7 compile/API fix

Minecraft 1.21.10 official Mojang mappings do not expose Vec2.multiply(float) or LocalPlayer.shouldSlowDown() under the names used by V99.6.

Changes:
- Replaced Vec2.multiply(float) calls with explicit Vec2 construction.
- Replaced shouldSlowDown() calls with shadowed intermediary method_20303().
- Replaced ClientInput.hasForwardMovement() use with getMoveVector().y > 0.
- No intended behavior change beyond resolving the 1.21.10 mapping/API mismatch.
