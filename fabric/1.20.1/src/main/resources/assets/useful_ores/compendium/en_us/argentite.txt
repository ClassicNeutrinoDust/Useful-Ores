Argentite is the mod's silver ore, a basic low-tier material.

Found: Overworld, in stone only (no deepslate variant), from Y 0 to 64. Veins of 6, 8 per chunk. Ore touching air always spawns.

Drops: 1 Raw Argentite per ore block, increased by Fortune. Silk Touch drops the ore block itself.

Tool stats: durability 380, mining speed 5.0, attack damage +6.5, enchantability 18. Mining tier: Iron.

Perks: Argentite Ingots craft the [Argentite Filter](Redstone/Argentite Filter), a five-slot filtering hopper. Each slot only accepts items that match a reference stack, and you can set a reserve amount that automation cannot pull below.
