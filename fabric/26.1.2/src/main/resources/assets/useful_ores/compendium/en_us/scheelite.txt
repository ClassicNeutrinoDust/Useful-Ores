Scheelite is the mod's tungsten ore. It is best known for its arrow and its chisel.

Found: Overworld, in stone only (no deepslate variant), from Y 0 to 64. Veins of 6, 8 per chunk. Ore touching air always spawns.

Drops: 1 Raw Scheelite per ore block, increased by Fortune. Silk Touch drops the ore block itself.

Tool stats: durability 900, mining speed 6.0, attack damage +6.5, enchantability 10. Mining tier: Iron.

Perks: the [Scheelite Arrow](Tools & Combat/Special Arrows) looks and flies like a vanilla arrow but deals 1.5x the damage of a normal unenchanted bow shot.

Scheelite Ingots also craft the Scheelite Chisel, a 200 durability tool that turns compatible full blocks into slabs, and the Titanium Lock and Key pair.
