A surface structure with a scorched crater and a meteorite buried beneath it. Lonsdaleite ore is found inside the meteorite.

It generates on land in the Overworld, on average about once every 81 chunks, with at least 20 chunks between sites. Look for a burnt, bowl-shaped crater. The meteorite is an irregular mass 5–10 blocks in radius, with a denser Meteorite Core near the center.

Six Lonsdaleite ore pockets generate inside the meteorite. The core can be used to start the Meteor Staff and Meteor Bomb crafting chains.
