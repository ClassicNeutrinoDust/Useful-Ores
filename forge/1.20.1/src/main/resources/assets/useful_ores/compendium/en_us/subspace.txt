Subspace is the endgame material. Its tools mine far faster than anything else in the mod, and it is the only material that does not come from world generation.

Obtained: craft a Subspace Ingot with a [Heated Shard](Utility/Heated Shard) in the center of a ring of eight Enderium Ingots. There is no ore or vein. Subspace comes purely from crafting, after the top trio.

Tool stats: durability 6400, mining speed 32.0, attack damage +16.0, enchantability 34. All four are the highest in the mod. Mining tier: Netherite.

Perks: Subspace tools can be combined with a Farseeker Crystal to send mined blocks straight into your inventory.

Subspace items can also be combined with potions. The full armor set permanently gives its bound potion effect while worn. Subspace weapons give you a beneficial effect and the target a harmful one for 10 seconds on hit.
