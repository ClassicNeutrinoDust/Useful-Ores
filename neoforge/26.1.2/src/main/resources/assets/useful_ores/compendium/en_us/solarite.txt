Solarite is a radiant precious metal and the first of the top trio (Solarite, Enderium, Voidshard). It still mines noticeably faster than Netherite.

Found: The Nether, in netherrack, from Y 8 to 119. It is rare: veins of 3, 1 per chunk. Ore touching air never spawns, so veins that border open caverns come out smaller.

Drops: 1 Raw Solarite per ore block, increased by Fortune. Silk Touch drops the ore block itself.

Tool stats: durability 2400, mining speed 15.0, attack damage +12.0, enchantability 24. Mining tier: Netherite.

Perks: Solarite powers the [Solarite Furnace](Utility/Solarite Furnace), a 2x2x2 multiblock that smelts 5x faster than a Blast Furnace. It runs on direct sunlight or a charged Solar Battery.

Solarite is also the key ingredient of the self-propelled Solarite Battery Minecart and the Solarite Rail.
