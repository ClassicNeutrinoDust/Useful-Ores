Three arrows with different effects. They work with any bow and need no special charge.

Scheelite Arrow: deals 1.5 times the damage of a normal, unenchanted bow shot. Its flight and drop are otherwise the same as a vanilla arrow.

Arcanite Arrow: deals no direct damage. A hit gives the target one golden absorption heart, which remains until it absorbs damage.

Nyxium Arrow: takes up to one heart from the target, never reducing it below half a heart, and gives the shooter a golden absorption heart.
