Voidshard is the top of the trio and the strongest ore-based material in the mod below Subspace. It mines noticeably faster than Enderium.

Found: The End, in end stone, from Y 0 to 20, evenly distributed. Veins of 4. It is rare: only 1 in 5 chunks gets a vein.

Drops: 1 Raw Voidshard per ore block, increased by Fortune. Silk Touch drops the ore block itself.

Tool stats: durability 3000, mining speed 25.0, attack damage +15.0, enchantability 28. Mining tier: Netherite.

Best for: the strongest gear you can get through normal ore progression. Voidshard armor also has the highest toughness of the trio, which makes it a good step before Subspace.
