Argentite is the mod's silver ore - a solid, dependable low tier material.

Generation: Overworld, in stone only (it has no deepslate variant). It generates from Y 0 to Y 64, in clusters of 6, with 8 veins per chunk, and never disappears when exposed to air.

Drops: 1 Raw Argentite per block, boosted by Fortune, or the ore block itself when mined with Silk Touch.

Tool stats: 380 durability, 5.0 mining speed, +6.5 attack damage bonus, enchantability 18. It is not correct for iron-tier-only blocks.

Special ability: Argentite Ingots build the [Argentite Filter](Redstone/Argentite Filter), a five-slot filtered hopper that only accepts items matching a reference stack placed in each slot, with a protected reserve amount that automation cannot pull below.
