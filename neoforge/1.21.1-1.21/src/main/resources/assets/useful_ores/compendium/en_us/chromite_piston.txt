A Chromite piston and sticky piston work like their vanilla versions, but can also push Obsidian and Crying Obsidian. The sticky piston can pull those blocks back.

Place and wire them like ordinary pistons. Timing, redstone triggers and structure limits are unchanged.

Only Obsidian and Crying Obsidian gain this support. Blocks that are otherwise immovable, such as Respawn Anchors and Reinforced Deepslate, still cannot be moved. Vanilla push limits and block-entity restrictions still apply.

Craft the piston with planks, deepslate, redstone and a Chromite Ingot. Combine it with a Slime Ball to make the sticky version.
